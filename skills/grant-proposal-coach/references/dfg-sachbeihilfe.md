# DFG Sachbeihilfe (Research Grants), incl. Eigene Stelle
Source: DFG forms 50.01, 54.01, 53.01, 10.206 (09/26), 52.02, 53.200, 55.01. Check dfg.de for newer versions.

## Fit check
- Researchers in Germany with a completed doctorate, any career stage. No deadlines. Up to 3 years, renewable. No budget ceiling; every item must follow from the work programme.
- Eigene Stelle (own position): employer statement (41.027) required; not for professors or junior professors. No parallel Walter Benjamin, Emmy Noether or Heisenberg proposal.
- Permanent staff of MPG, FhG, Helmholtz and some Leibniz institutes must apply with a university partner (55.01); temporary staff are exempt. Eigene Stelle there: alone only within 6 years of the doctorate and if the institute covers 45% of project costs.
- Better fit: own group and professorship goal within about 4 years of the doctorate: Emmy Noether. Stay abroad: Feodor Lynen.
- Ask: doctorate date; contract type and end date; institution type; Eigene Stelle or not; pending Walter Benjamin, Emmy Noether or Heisenberg proposals; first-time proposal; duration.

## Template (53.01) and page budget
Project description max. 25 pages: sections 1–3 max. 17, section 4 onwards max. 8. German or English. Suggested split of the 17 (not a DFG rule): section 1 about 5–6, section 2 about 9–10, section 3 about 2.
1. Starting point (Ausgangslage): state of the art and preliminary work (Stand der Forschung und eigene Vorarbeiten). Show "a unique, innovative, promising contribution"; "concise and understandable without referring to additional literature".
2. Objectives and work programme (Ziele und Arbeitsprogramm)
   2.1 Anticipated total duration
   2.2 Objectives (also state any relevance outside science)
   2.3 Work programme incl. proposed research methods: steps, schedule, existing and new methods. "The quality of the work programme is critical to the success of a funding proposal." Tasks "should correspond to the funds requested".
   2.4 Handling of research data
   2.5 Relevance of sex, gender and/or diversity (in the research content, not the team)
3. Project- and subject-related list of publications: only works cited in 1–2; max. 10 own works highlighted per proposal; own and others' work clearly marked.
4. Supplementary information (Begleitinformationen zum Forschungskontext): ethics; employment status; first-time proposal; project group; cooperation partners; collaborators of the last 3 years; equipment; other submissions.
5. Requested modules/funds (Beantragte Module/Mittel): every item justified (within the 8 pages).
Also: summary in German and English; CV (form 53.200), max. 4 pages, max. 10 Category A and 10 Category B outputs; no impact factors or h-indices.

## What reviewers are asked (10.206)
1. "…quality of the project, especially with regard to originality and the anticipated contribution to knowledge gain?"
2. "…clear working hypotheses and an appropriately distinct topic… the suitability of the methods and the appropriateness of the schedule… handling of research data?"
3. "…soundness of the preliminary work, the quality of publications… qualified to independently lead the proposed project?" Judged against career stage.
4. "…work and research environment at the institution."
5. Whether "the requested funds are justified and reasonable".
Usually two written reviews; the review board (Fachkollegium) ranks all proposals in its field. Success rate under 25%.

## Extra interview topics
One working hypothesis per objective; how the topic is narrowed; schedule per work package; data access; what each requested person does.

## Pitfalls (DFG's own rejection reasons)
- Doubtful added value; question already answered in essential aspects.
- Unclear work programme; unsuitable methods; feasibility or expertise in doubt.
- Funds not derived from the work programme.
- "What is not named in the CV or proposal is not taken into account."

## AI disclosure: required
If generative AI was used for text or images, disclose in the proposal which models, for what purpose and to what extent (54.01). A few sentences suffice. Grammar, style, spelling and translation tools are exempt; text drafted with this coach is not. No section is prescribed: ask the DFG where to put it.
