# DFG Emmy Noether Programme
Source: DFG forms 50.02, 54.01, 53.05, 10.210 (09/26), 53.200, 53.12. Check dfg.de for newer versions.

## Fit check
- Aim: qualify for a professorship within six years by independently leading an Emmy Noether Group, with some teaching.
- Excellent doctorate and strong publications.
- As a rule up to 4 years since the doctorate at submission (6 for licensed medicine and psychology applicants). Childcare extends this: 2 years per child for women; 1 for men, up to 2 with proof of care; total extension max. 6 years; children under 12 at the start of the window, living in the household. The window starts at the date on the doctoral certificate. Compute it explicitly, with and without proof of care, and say which result applies.
- As a rule 2 years of postdoc experience that produced scientific independence.
- Substantial international research experience, described in a separate appendix.
- Excluded: habilitated or equivalent; positively evaluated junior professors; leaders of a comparable funded group.
- Host in Germany; not the university of the doctorate unless well justified.
- 6 years, requested as 3 + 3; interim evaluation at the end of year 3. No deadlines. Success rate about 16–20%.
- Better fit: no group or professorship goal, or outside the window: Sachbeihilfe with Eigene Stelle. Stay abroad: Feodor Lynen. Up to 10 years after the PhD: ERC Starting Grant.
- Ask: doctorate date and children; postdoc years; place of doctorate and planned host(s); international experience; Habilitation or junior professorship.

## Template (53.05) and page budget
Project description max. 25 pages: sections 1–3 max. 17, section 4 onwards max. 8. German or English. Suggested split of the 17 (not a DFG rule): section 1 about 5, section 2 about 10, section 3 about 2.
1. Starting point: state of the art and preliminary work
2. Objectives and work programme: anticipated total duration; objectives; work programme incl. methods (3 + 3); handling of research data; relevance of sex, gender and/or diversity; justification of each host institution
3. Project- and subject-related list of publications: max. 10 own works highlighted; own work clearly marked
4. onwards: ethics; project group; cooperation partners; equipment; other submissions; requested modules, justified item by item
Attachments: CV (53.200, max. 4 pages, max. 10 Category A and 10 Category B outputs; no impact factors or h-indices); doctoral certificate; employer's statement or model contract (53.12) per host; description of international research experience.

## What reviewers are asked (10.210)
1. "the soundness of the preliminary work, the quality of publications … and the qualifications of the applicant"
2. "Does the applicant have the ability to independently lead an Emmy Noether Group? How would you assess the applicant's chances of being appointed to a professorship after the Emmy Noether Group ends?"
3. "originality and the anticipated contribution to knowledge gain"
4. Clear working hypotheses and a sensibly narrowed topic; methods; schedule; research data
5. Suitability of every host institution listed
6. A clear recommendation for or against funding; an itemised budget proposal

## Extra interview topics
- Independence: how the research line differs from the former supervisor's; what the applicant alone initiated.
- Leadership: planned group, doctoral supervision, teaching (model contract: 2 SWS), path to a professorship.
- Why each host, and why not the university of the doctorate.
- What must be reached by year 3; what years 4–6 add.

## Pitfalls (derived from the criteria; the DFG publishes no list)
- Project continues the supervisor's programme.
- No plan for supervision, team or teaching.
- Topic too broad; no 3 + 3 structure; staff not derived from the work.
- Host justified generically; employer's statement missing.
- Personal presentation: current forms do not mention one; older sources conflict. Unconfirmed; ask the DFG.

## AI disclosure: required
If generative AI was used for text or images, disclose in the proposal which models, for what purpose and to what extent (54.01). A few sentences suffice. Grammar, style, spelling and translation tools are exempt; text drafted with this coach is not.
