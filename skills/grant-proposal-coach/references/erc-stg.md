# ERC Starting Grant
Source: ERC Work Programme 2027; Information for Applicants StG/CoG 2027. That call closed on 14 October 2026; rules for the next call are not yet published.

## Fit check
- PhD defended more than 0 and at most 10 years before 1 January of the call year (2027 call). Documented extensions, e.g. 18 months per child for maternity, parental leave, long illness, clinical training.
- Potential for independence, e.g. "at least one important publication as its main author or a publication without the participation of their PhD supervisor".
- Up to EUR 1.5 million for up to 5 years. Host in an EU Member State or Associated Country, with a binding support letter. At least 50% of working time on the project.
- One main-grant proposal per Work Programme; one Starting Grant per career. A B or C at Step 1 in 2026 barred the PI from the 2027 calls; later rules are unpublished.
- About 1 in 20 funded (2027 call).
- Better fit: solid but not frontier-level idea, or thin record: DFG Sachbeihilfe (Eigene Stelle). Stay abroad: Feodor Lynen.
- Ask: PhD defence date and extensions; host; publications without the PhD supervisor; earlier ERC results; panel.

## Template and page budget
At least 11 pt. Limits are "strictly applied".
- Part B1 (all that Step 1 reads):
  - Cover page with abstract (half a page; not counted)
  - Part I of the Scientific Proposal, max. 5 pages excl. references. It must "(1) lay out the current state of knowledge, (2) explain the scientific question and the objectives of the project and (3) present the overall approach or research strategy". It must stand alone. No feasibility detail. Say early "what may be changed, opened, challenged or how the results of the work will alter the current understanding of the field" (IfA 2027). Suggested split (not an ERC rule): state of knowledge 1–1.5, question and objectives 1.5, strategy 2.
  - CV and Track Record, max. 4 pages: up to ten research outputs, each with an optional note on significance and own role; peer recognition.
- Part B2 (Step 2 only): Part II, max. 7 pages excl. references: "research methodology, work plan, risk assessment, and mitigating measures". It "should not be a repetition of Part I". Suggested split: methodology 4, work plan 1.5, risks 1.5.
- Part A: Resources and Time Commitment (budget table; text max. 8,000 characters).

## Criterion and questions (Work Programme 2027)
"Excellence is the sole criterion of evaluation."
Project, Step 1: "To what extent does the research address important scientific questions?" "To what extent are the project's objectives ambitious, will the project advance the frontier of knowledge and is the proposed approach scientifically convincing?"
Added at Step 2: "To what extent are the research methodology and working arrangements appropriate to achieve the goals of the project?" "To what extent are the timescales and resources adequate and properly justified?"
PI: "To what extent has the PI demonstrated the ability to conduct ground-breaking research?" "…evidence of creative and original thinking?" "…the required scientific expertise and capacity to successfully execute the project?"
Step 1 panel members read as generalists; outcomes A, B, C. Step 2: specialist reviews and an interview of about 30 minutes.

## Extra interview topics
What changes in the field if the project works; why it is more than the next step; independence from the PhD supervisor; how each research output advanced the field; team and budget per objective.

## Pitfalls (talks by ERC panel members; the ERC publishes no statistics)
- Incremental, or recycled from another funder's proposal.
- Hypothesis and objectives unclear; no stated breakthrough.
- Part I does not stand alone; Part II repeats it.
- Risk ignored or overpromised; no contingency.
- PI section underplayed or oversold; resources inconsistent with Part II.

## AI disclosure: not required
The ERC does not ban AI use and requires no disclosure (Scientific Council position, updated 03/2026). The applicant keeps "full and sole authorship responsibilities".
