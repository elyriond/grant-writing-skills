# Sources

The reviewers and the writing coach are built from the funders' own documents, as of November 2026. Rules change with every call — always upload the current versions.

## ERC Starting Grant
- [ERC Work Programme 2027](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/docs/2021-2027/horizon/wp-call/2027/wp_horizon-erc-2027_en.pdf) — evaluation criterion and questions, eligibility, Step 1 / Step 2
- [Information for Applicants, StG/CoG](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/docs/2021-2027/horizon/guidance/information-for-applicants_he-erc-stg-cog_en.pdf) — page limits, templates, PI rating scale, interview
- [Use of AI in grant proposal evaluation (March 2026)](https://erc.europa.eu/system/files/2026-03/Use-AI-grant-proposal-evaluation.pdf) and [ERC news](https://erc.europa.eu/news-events/news/erc-clarifies-limits-ai-use-grant-evaluation)
- [Starting Grants 2027 applications: facts and figures](https://erc.europa.eu/news-events/news/starting-grants-2027-applications-facts-and-figures)

## Known gaps
- The DFG publishes no grading scale; the reviewers give a recommendation, not a score.
- Whether Emmy Noether currently includes a personal presentation could not be confirmed.
- No funder publishes statistics on reasons for rejection. The "known failure modes" come from the official criteria and from talks by ERC panel members and university research offices.
