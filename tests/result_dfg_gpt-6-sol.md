## Three things you can build on

These are **starting assets, not established strengths of the proposal**:

1. **Berlin parks are a concrete potential study setting.** Turn that into a defensible site-selection and comparison design; “Berlin parks” alone is not one.
2. **You report relevant group publications.** Their value is in the findings, methods and *your documented contribution*, not journal metrics.
3. **The 36-month duration permits repeated seasonal sampling.** The current schedule does not yet show how you will use that opportunity.

**The hardest unanswered question:** *What specific uncertainty about urban pollinators can this design resolve that existing urban-ecology studies have not already resolved?* Until you can answer that in the opening pages, more detail on fieldwork will not save the proposal.
