This is a review of a **very partial project description plus a budget outline**, not a reviewable full Sachbeihilfe proposal. I can assess the visible sections, but not whether the actual research question is original, whether the methods are sound, or whether your track record supports the project. ## Main problems, ranked

1. **There is no research question or testable hypothesis.**  
   “Explore the role of urban green spaces for pollinators” and “generate new insights into urban biodiversity” do not specify what you will explain, compare, or predict. A reviewer cannot judge originality or anticipated knowledge gain—and cannot tell whether the question has already been answered.  
   **Hits:** project quality; objectives and work programme.  
   **Repair:** State one bounded question and explicit hypotheses with predicted outcomes. Define the pollinator group, the specific urban-green-space feature or contrast, and the ecological process at issue. Explain what the project will resolve that existing work has not.

2. **The work programme is not a research design.**  
   “We will collect data on pollinators,” “standard methods,” and “statistical analyses … as appropriate” leave out the sampling unit, site selection, replication, sampling frequency, response variables, predictors, analytical approach, and how these connect to the hypotheses. There is no basis for judging whether the design can answer the question.  
   **Hits:** objectives and work programme; schedule; preliminary work.  
   **Repair:** Give a design that maps each hypothesis to measurements, sampling, and analysis. Justify sample size or replication, address seasonal variation, and specify permits and access to the Berlin parks. Add foreseeable failure points and fallback plans.

3. **The claimed preliminary work is attributed to the group, not demonstrably to you.**  
   “Our group has published extensively” does not establish your contribution or your ability to lead an independent project. Listing a *Nature* paper by impact factor makes this worse: **impact factors are not a criterion and must not be used to support the quality of your work**.  
   **Hits:** qualifications; soundness of preliminary work; independence for Eigene Stelle.  
   **Repair:** Identify your own relevant contributions and what they establish for this project. In the CV and proposal, make your role and the distinction between your work and other people’s work clear. The full publication record and career-stage context are needed to judge this properly.

4. **The application is formally and administratively incomplete.**  
   The summary is English-only; a German summary is also required. The research-data section is missing. You say **12 own works are highlighted**, exceeding the maximum of 10 highlighted own works per proposal. You also state that parts of section 1 were drafted with ChatGPT but have no AI disclosure.  
   **Hits:** formal compliance; research-data handling; credibility.  
   **Repair:** Add the German summary and a concrete research-data plan covering data types, documentation, storage, access, sharing, and preservation. Reduce highlighted own works to at most 10 and clearly distinguish your work from others’. Disclose the generative-AI use as required, naming the model, purpose, and extent; “ChatGPT” alone may not identify the model/version adequately.

5. **The draft gives a reviewer little reason to rank it above competing proposals.**  
   “Many studies have looked at pollinators in cities” acknowledges a large literature but does not locate a gap. “The landscape of research is broad and complex” adds no evidence. Without a focused gap, the added value is invisible to a broad Fachkollegium member.  
   **Hits:** originality and knowledge gain; comparative ranking.  
   **Repair:** Use a short, evidence-based account of the closest work, identify the unresolved point, and state the specific advance your design will deliver. The bibliography was not provided, so I cannot assess whether the literature coverage supports that claim.

## Other issues a reviewer would flag

The opening reads as generic promotional prose: **“it is no secret,” “truly pivotal,”** and “rapidly evolving landscape” assert importance instead of establishing the project’s problem. The impact-factor claim is particularly damaging because it relies on a metric reviewers should not use. Replace this with a precise ecological problem and a verifiable knowledge gap.

The schedule is only a set of broad phases. A six-month literature review followed by 18 months of unspecified fieldwork does not show that the sampling fits the relevant seasons, that analyses can be completed in time, or that the project can deliver within 36 months. The timing needs to follow from the actual design.

For an Eigene Stelle at a Leibniz institute, include the required **employer statement**. Your fixed-term status does not appear to trigger the stated rule for permanent staff at non-university institutes, but confirm the cooperation requirements for your exact institutional and employment status with the host and the current DFG forms.
