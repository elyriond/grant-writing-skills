**Material reviewed:** a project-description summary and CV notes, not a full proposal. I am treating this as your own draft. I have not seen uploaded DFG forms; my knowledge refers to the 09/26 versions and may be outdated.

## Potentially fatal weaknesses

1. **The independence case currently fails.** You describe the project as “extending the Müller framework,” say it “continues its line of research,” have spent your PhD and entire postdoc in Prof. Müller’s group, and propose the same department as host. His mentorship and cluster access reinforce the impression that this would remain his programme. A review board could reasonably ask why this is an independent Emmy Noether Group rather than an expansion of his group. **Repair:** define a research question and intellectual approach that are demonstrably yours; distinguish your contributions to the framework from his; specify your decision-making, resources and scientific boundaries with his group. Give a substantive reason for Heidelberg as host—and address directly why staying at your PhD institution is necessary.

2. **The work programme is broader than its scientific question.** Six goals run from adaptation and validation to a web tool, industry collaboration and exploratory machine learning. The summary gives no working hypotheses, narrowing of target systems, or preliminary result showing that the riskiest adaptation is feasible. **Repair:** state the knowledge claim the membrane-protein work will test, identify the systems and validation criteria, and separate essential experiments from optional extensions. Show preliminary evidence for the highest-risk step.

3. **The duration and group request do not follow from the plan.** A five-year Gantt chart does not explain a six-year Emmy Noether Group structured as 3 + 3 years. Three PhD students, two postdocs and a technician are a substantial request without assigned tasks or a supervision plan. **Repair:** build a six-year programme with a credible first three-year phase and decision points for years 4–6; link each position, its duration and its work to specific tasks. Explain how you will supervise and build the group, and what teaching you will undertake.

4. **The record does not yet demonstrate independence or substantial international experience.** Nine papers with Prof. Müller as last author do not negate your contribution, but journal impact factors cannot establish it. A two-month ETH visit is unlikely, on its own, to make a persuasive case for substantial international research experience. **Repair:** explain your own contribution and intellectual ownership paper by paper; identify outputs or collaborations that show a distinct trajectory. In the separate international-experience appendix, say what the ETH visit produced and disclose any other relevant experience. Do not imply that two months automatically satisfies the expectation.

## How reviewers are likely to read this

A specialist may see a feasible extension of an existing computational framework, but will look for a sharper hypothesis and evidence that it works for membrane proteins. A review board comparing applicants is likely to see a capable member of Prof. Müller’s group whose transition to independent leadership has not yet been demonstrated. Being 3.5 years after the doctorate and having postdoctoral experience do not resolve that central issue.

**Repair priority:** settle the independence and host-institution case *before* polishing the Gantt chart or adding applications. If the project cannot be credibly separated from Prof. Müller’s programme, better presentation will not fix it.

**Hardest unanswered question:** *What scientific programme will you lead that Prof. Müller would not otherwise lead, and what in your record shows that you—not his group—own it?*

**Verdict on this material:** It currently **hurts** the application. On the information supplied, I would expect a **do not fund** recommendation, principally because independence is unconvincing; the overextended work plan and unsupported group request compound the problem.
