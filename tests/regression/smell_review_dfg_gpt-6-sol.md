### Potentially fatal weaknesses, in repair order

1. **There is no assessable research question.** “Explore the role of urban green spaces for pollinators” does not identify a gap, a working hypothesis, a comparison or an anticipated gain in knowledge. Your section 1 even says “many studies have looked at pollinators in cities” without explaining what remains unanswered. This hits **project quality and objectives**. State what existing work cannot establish, then specify the competing explanation or prediction your study can test. Until you do, a reviewer cannot tell whether the question has already been answered.

2. **The work programme cannot support the objective as written.** “We will collect data” and “standard methods” specify neither sites and access, sampling design, taxa and outcomes, controls, seasonality, sample size, nor analysis. WP3 postpones analysis until month 25, leaving no visible opportunity to correct a failed design or season of fieldwork. This hits **methods, feasibility and schedule**. Derive each WP from a hypothesis; give the site-selection logic, methods, analysis plan, timetable, dependencies and fall-backs. Write section 2.3 on data handling. Address ethics or permissions where applicable.

3. **The claimed preliminary-work and independence case is unsupported.** “Our group has published extensively” does not establish *your* contribution or ability to lead independently. Journal impact factors are not an assessment criterion. This hits **applicant qualifications**, particularly for *Eigene Stelle*. Identify your own relevant results and role in the papers, show which methods and access are already established, and explain what you will direct independently. The CV and actual publications could change this judgement; they are not supplied.

4. **Institutional feasibility and the Eigene-Stelle conditions are not demonstrated.** A Berlin field programme needs credible access and an account of available equipment and support. Supply the research-environment information and required employer statement. At a non-university institute, clarify the application arrangement and institutional contribution applicable to your fixed-term position; the six-years-after-doctorate condition makes the stated **45% institutional coverage for an independent Eigene-Stelle application there** a question you must resolve before submission. Do not assume that the university-partner rule for *permanent* non-university staff automatically describes your fixed-term case.

5. **Several visible formal defects are avoidable.** Twelve highlighted own works exceed the limit of **ten per proposal**. The summary needs German **and** English. Because ChatGPT drafted parts of section 1, disclose the model, purpose and extent of that text use; an absent AI-use section does not do so. These are separate from—and easier to fix than—the scientific deficiencies.

### How reviewers are likely to read this

A specialist cannot judge novelty, methodological validity or whether three years and the requested staff are necessary: the testable study is missing. A Fachkollegium member comparing proposals will see the broad claim immediately but no reason to rank this project above a specified, feasible pollinator study. The **hardest unanswered question** is: *What will your Berlin data establish that existing urban-pollinator studies cannot?*
