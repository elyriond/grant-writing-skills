## Fatal weaknesses and repair priorities

1. **The research question is missing.** “Continues my doctoral thesis … and extends the same analysis to the US” describes a location change, not an original question. A field reviewer will ask what comparing Germany and the US can establish that the German study could not. State the specific question, the expected contribution to sociology, and why the US comparison is necessary to answer it.

2. **The 24-month plan is not credible as written.** Replicating a survey, conducting 60 interviews, building a database, developing a theory, writing a monograph *and* organising a conference are six ambitions without a sequence or feasibility argument. Identify the essential research outputs, explain access to respondents and data, and show what can realistically be completed during the stay.

3. **The methods do not support the comparative claim yet.** “Survey and interviews, as in my thesis” does not address whether German and US samples, measures, platform-work categories and labour-market contexts are comparable. Say precisely what you will measure, how the two settings will be compared, and what the interviews add that the survey cannot establish.

4. **The academic host’s role is generic.** “World-leading” and “excellent resources” could describe many hosts. You met Prof. Smith once; the outline does not show a developed intellectual connection or why this research is viable at Stanford. Name the host’s relevant expertise or access and the specific contribution of the host institute. Confirm that Prof. Smith qualifies under the **current eligible-host categories**—being a former Humboldt Research Award winner should not be dismissed, but the exact eligibility route needs checking. The host’s statement will also need to explain how the proposal arose and whether you developed it independently.

5. **Both documents obscure your own contribution.** The key-publication note says the article was co-authored “with my supervisor and two colleagues” but never says which ideas, analyses or writing were yours. That is a direct weakness under the key-publication criterion. Replace claims such as “leading journal,” “cited several times” and “demonstrates its impact” with your identifiable intellectual contribution and what is original about the finding.
