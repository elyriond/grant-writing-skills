1. Starting point
In today's rapidly evolving landscape of urban ecology, it is no secret that pollinators are crucial. Urban green spaces are truly pivotal for biodiversity. Our group has published extensively on this topic, including a paper in Nature (impact factor 64.8) and two papers in journals with impact factors above 10, which demonstrates the high quality of our preliminary work. Many studies have looked at pollinators in cities, and the landscape of research is broad and complex.

2. Objectives and work programme
2.1 Objectives: The project will explore the role of urban green spaces for pollinators and generate new insights into urban biodiversity.

WP1: Literature review (months 1–6).
WP2: Field work in Berlin parks (months 7–24). We will collect data on pollinators.
WP3: Analysis and publication (months 25–36).
